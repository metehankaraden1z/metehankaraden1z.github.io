☆ About Me ☆

I'm a Computer Science student at Bilkent University in Ankara, currently working on Java, Python, and C++.

This site exists so people who search my name find something more interesting than a LinkedIn profile or a forgotten Facebook account.

I was born on 22/08/2006 and graduated from Şehit Oğuzhan Yaşar Anadolu Lisesi before studying Computer Science at Bilkent University. What made me think about choosing the CS department was my first experience of creating a Hamachi server in Minecraft. Outside of coding, my hobbies include watching pro wrestling (mainly WWE), watching and playing football, and playing sports, FPS, and story-based video games. I also love rap and mainly listen to Eminem's music.

Since starting Computer Science, I have worked on two projects, which can be seen on my GitHub page below. My first project, SeeMore, is a traveling guide application, and my second project, Crescendo, is a music discovery and review application. Both of these applications were written in Java.
